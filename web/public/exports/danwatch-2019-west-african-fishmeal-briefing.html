Danish investigative outlet Danwatch's series on fishmeal and fish oil imported from West Africa. In October 2019 Danwatch put questions to six Danish companies buying fishmeal and fish oil from the region about how they avoid affecting West African food security. BioMar was among the companies named, alongside Pelagia, Triple Nine Group, FF Skagen, Aller Aqua and ED&F Man.
